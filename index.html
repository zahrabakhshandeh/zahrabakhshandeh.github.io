About

I work with data, with a special focus on healthcare. I clean and analyse data, store it in databases, and turn it into charts, and I also build AI models that learn from it. I’ve worked with financial, retail, education and healthcare data, but healthcare is really my interest.

I have over 3 years of experience in AI in healthcare with the BitTwin team, where we spent our time finding gaps, developing real-world solutions, working with image, video and text data, and writing proposals on how AI could help address healthcare gaps. This experience is the reason I chose to do a PhD in Computer Science at the University of Westminster, focusing on AI in healthcare. My research uses AI to analyse medical images, videos and speech to predict dementia.

Alongside AI in healthcare, I’m passionate about teaching what I learn, because it gives me a sense of purpose and deepens my own understanding. I have over 5 years of teaching experience, covering Python, databases and data analysis for university students, online learners and medical students. Teaching is a big part of my life :)

I’m now based in London, and I’d love to connect with people working on healthcare projects, data science, AI and programming education. I also enjoy workshops and meetups, as they’re a great way to learn, meet new people and exchange ideas.
